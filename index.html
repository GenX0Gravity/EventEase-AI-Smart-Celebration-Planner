Connects to your local Ollama server. 100% private, free, and runs on your machine.

Quick Ollama Setup (Free & Open Source)

  1. Download Ollama from ollama.ai
  2. In your terminal, pull a model: ollama run qwen2.5:3b
  3. To allow browser connections, launch with CORS enabled: OLLAMA_ORIGINS="*" ollama serve
